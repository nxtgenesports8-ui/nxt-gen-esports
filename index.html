Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Slot Cancellation

Cancellation and refund requests will be handled manually by the NXT GEN ESPORTS team. Contact support before the scheduled match whenever possible.

2. Organiser Cancellation

If a match is cancelled or cannot be conducted because of an organiser-side issue, affected teams may contact support for the applicable refund or rescheduling process.

3. Failure to Join

If a team fails to join a lobby on time for personal or team-side reasons, a refund may not be available.

4. Technical Issues

Internet, device, power, ping, or other player-side technical issues are normally the player's responsibility.

5. Fair Play

Cheating, hacking, teaming, exploits or prohibited software can result in disqualification and forfeiture of the entry fee.

6. Contact Support

For cancellations, refunds or support, contact NXT GEN ESPORTS through WhatsApp at +91 9045524457. A second support number will be added when you provide it.
